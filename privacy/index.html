Prism Duel privacy policy

Privacy policy — 11 November 2026. Published at https://hory80.github.io/prismduel-site/privacy/. Support: rosca.apps@gmail.com.

The game works without analytics

Prism Duel is a single-player puzzle duel. No account, advertising, cross-app tracking, camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access is required. Progress, earned coins, cosmetic equipment, settings, local daily-run dates and best scores are stored on your device. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your device backups. Online gameplay and cloud campaign saves are not included in the current version. The online trial allowance uses iCloud key-value storage when available.

Optional analytics and crash reports

“Share anonymous analytics” is off by default. First launch offers equally prominent Yes and No thanks choices; either leads to the same game. You can change your choice in Settings. If you turn it on and the developer has configured the service, Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help us understand game balance and diagnose failures. Without that configuration nothing is sent by this feature. Turning it off stops future collection, resets the local analytics identifier/data and discards unsent crash reports; it does not promise to erase reports already received by the service.

Events describe tutorial progress, match starts/results, rival and stage, move counts, remaining HP, stars, active match duration, combos, specials, Prism Slash uses, Rush score/tier, unlocked stages, setting changes and earned-coin cosmetic purchases. With consent, purchase-related events include the paywall source, purchase start/success/failure category, restore requests and the number of online trials used. A feedback event contains only a rating and whether a written note exists. We never include the note, an email address, a player name or a custom user ID in analytics.

Firebase may process app-instance/installation identifiers, app and operating-system versions, device characteristics, crash stacks and approximate geographic information derived from network connections. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not a guarantee of irreversible anonymity. We use the Analytics Core product without advertising-identifier support, disable IDFV collection and advertising consent/personalization, and do not use analytics for advertising or cross-company tracking. IP addresses are needed to deliver network requests; the game never requests your GPS location.

Google operates the processing service. See Firebase privacy and security and Google’s privacy policy. Analytics event and user data retention is set to two months, with reset on new activity disabled (verified 11 November 2026). This does not limit most aggregated standard reports. Firebase Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems, under Google’s documented policy. Access to dashboards is restricted to the developer. Data may be processed outside your country.

Optional Full Game purchase and online trials

Worlds 1–2, their Prism Rush, Wardrobe and Bazaar are free. The optional non-consumable Full Game purchase unlocks Worlds 3–6 and unlimited future online duels. Apple processes payment; we do not receive your payment-card details. Once configured, RevenueCat processes store transactions, purchase history, entitlement status and an anonymous app-specific identifier to verify, restore and manage access. This essential purchase processing and RevenueCat purchase reporting are separate from optional Firebase gameplay analytics and may occur with that consent off. No advertising identifiers, contact attributes or Firebase integration are supplied to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat privacy information and Apple privacy information.

The three free online trial matches share one allowance across async and live play. Random match identifiers marking used trials are stored locally and in your Apple iCloud key-value store. Removing the app does not reset synchronized usage when you use the same iCloud account. A fresh installation waits for cloud synchronization before admitting a trial; it does not affect offline free worlds. Invited matches verified as sponsored by a Full Game owner do not consume a trial. Campaign progress is not uploaded by this feature. Restore purchases uses your Apple Account; refunds or revoked access are applied when the store can be refreshed. Contact support about purchase/privacy requests. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat under their service and legal obligations; the two-month Firebase retention setting does not apply to them.

Feedback

The in-game feedback card stores at most 20 notes of up to 1,000 characters locally. You can delete them in Settings. An automatic invitation appears after three completed duels, then no more than once per seven days; it is optional. Settings lets you open feedback whenever you choose.

Support is available at rosca.apps@gmail.com. Saved notes are not automatically delivered to the developer. The Email feedback button opens a draft in your mail app; you decide whether to send it. Emailing voluntarily shares your message and sender address, and is used only to respond to your support request. Avoid personal or sensitive information in notes.

Your choices and contact

You can play with analytics off, switch it off later, delete saved feedback, and email rosca.apps@gmail.com for support. There is no advertising-tracking permission prompt. This game is not submitted in Apple’s Made for Kids category.

Contact rosca.apps@gmail.com for support or privacy requests; because the game does not collect your name or account, locating an individual anonymous/pseudonymous report may not be possible without additional information you choose to provide.
