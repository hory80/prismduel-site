Prism Duel privacy policy

Privacy policy — 11 November 2026. Published at https://hory80.github.io/prismduel-site/privacy/. Support: rosca.apps@gmail.com.

The game works without analytics

Prism Duel offers solo puzzle duels and optional Game Center online duels. No account, advertising, cross-app tracking, camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access is required. Progress, earned coins, cosmetic equipment, settings, local daily-run dates and best scores are stored on your device. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your device backups. Campaign saves remain local. The online trial allowance uses iCloud key-value storage when available.

Optional analytics and crash reports

“Share anonymous analytics” is off by default. First launch offers equally prominent Yes and No thanks choices; either leads to the same game. You can change your choice in Settings. If you turn it on and the developer has configured the service, Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help us understand game balance and diagnose failures. Without that configuration nothing is sent by this feature. Turning it off stops future collection, resets the local analytics identifier/data and discards unsent crash reports; it does not promise to erase reports already received by the service.

Events describe tutorial progress, match starts/results, rival and stage, move counts, remaining HP, stars, active match duration, combos, specials, Prism Slash uses, Rush score/tier, unlocked stages, setting changes and earned-coin cosmetic purchases. With consent, purchase-related events include the paywall source, purchase start/success/failure category, restore requests and the number of online trials used. A feedback event contains only a rating and whether a written note exists. We never include the note, an email address, a player name or a custom user ID in analytics.

Firebase may process app-instance/installation identifiers, app and operating-system versions, device characteristics, crash stacks and approximate geographic information derived from network connections. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not a guarantee of irreversible anonymity. We use the Analytics Core product without advertising-identifier support, disable IDFV collection and advertising consent/personalization, and do not use analytics for advertising or cross-company tracking. IP addresses are needed to deliver network requests; the game never requests your GPS location.

Google operates the processing service. See Firebase privacy and security and Google’s privacy policy. Analytics event and user data retention is set to two months, with reset on new activity disabled (verified 11 November 2026). This does not limit most aggregated standard reports. Firebase Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems, under Google’s documented policy. Access to dashboards is restricted to the developer. Data may be processed outside your country.

Optional Full Game in-app purchase and online trials

Worlds 1–2, their Prism Rush, Wardrobe and Bazaar are free. The optional non-consumable Full Game purchase unlocks Worlds 3–6 and unlimited online duels. Apple processes payments through StoreKit 2; we do not receive payment-card details. The app checks Apple's signed transactions on your device to verify ownership, restore purchases, and apply Family Sharing, refunds and revocations. No third-party purchase SDK or purchase-service account is used. Essential Apple purchase processing is separate from optional Firebase analytics. With analytics enabled, purchase/restore events may be recorded by Firebase; with it disabled the app does not send those analytics events. See Apple privacy information.

The three free online trial matches share one allowance across async and live play. Random match identifiers marking used trials are stored locally and in your Apple iCloud key-value store. Removing the app does not reset synchronized usage when you use the same iCloud account. A fresh installation waits for cloud synchronization before admitting a trial; it does not affect offline free worlds. Invited matches verified as sponsored by a Full Game owner do not consume a trial. Campaign progress is not uploaded by this feature. Restore purchases uses your Apple Account; refunds or revoked access are applied when the store can be refreshed. Contact support about purchase/privacy requests. Purchase records may be retained by Apple under its service and legal obligations; the two-month Firebase retention setting does not apply to them.

Game Center online duels

Online play is optional; solo worlds work without a Game Center account. Apple Game Center handles sign-in, matchmaking, friend invitations, turn notifications and match records. A record contains the board seed, ordered moves, integrity hashes, ruleset, participants’ Game Center identifiers/display names and equipped cape identifiers. Records are stored on Apple’s Game Center service for the match and retained according to Apple’s service controls; you can manage finished matches and your Game Center account through Apple. No chat is provided. Multiplayer and communication restrictions are respected.

The app does not send Game Center identifiers, names, match IDs or move records to Firebase analytics or crash reports. With optional analytics enabled, online events contain only mode/ruleset, aggregate move count, duration and result; a desync report contains no player data. Local admission and reward receipts prevent duplicate trial/reward claims.

For a friend invitation or rematch, each participant's device verifies its own Full Game entitlement with StoreKit 2 and shares the ownership status in the Game Center match record so either owner can cover the other player's access. No purchase customer identifier is sent to another purchase provider. The friend receives the sponsorship status, not transaction receipts or payment details. Expired invitation summaries may remain locally until removed. This is essential Game Center access processing, independent of optional gameplay analytics; no display name, email, contacts or payment details are sent to a third-party purchase service.

Feedback

The in-game feedback card stores at most 20 notes of up to 1,000 characters locally. You can delete them in Settings. An automatic invitation appears after three completed duels, then no more than once per seven days; it is optional. Settings lets you open feedback whenever you choose.

Support is available at rosca.apps@gmail.com. Saved notes are not automatically delivered to the developer. The Email feedback button opens a draft in your mail app; you decide whether to send it. Emailing voluntarily shares your message and sender address, and is used only to respond to your support request. Avoid personal or sensitive information in notes.

Your choices and contact

You can play with analytics off, switch it off later, delete saved feedback, and email rosca.apps@gmail.com for support. There is no advertising-tracking permission prompt. This game is not submitted in Apple’s Made for Kids category.

Contact rosca.apps@gmail.com for support or privacy requests; because optional analytics does not receive your Game Center identity, locating an individual anonymous/pseudonymous report may not be possible without additional information you choose to provide.

Game Center friends When you open Friends, Prism Duel asks Game Center for permission to show your Game Center friends' display names and profile photos so you can choose an exact opponent. Both friends must grant access in Prism Duel. This list is kept only in memory while the picker is open; refreshing removes friends whose access was revoked. We do not access your phone address book, upload a friend list to our own server, or use it for advertising. Add Friends uses Apple's own friend-request interface. You can change friend-list access in Settings → Game Center.
