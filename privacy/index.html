Prism Duel privacy policy

Beta privacy policy — 11 November 2026. A support mailbox is not configured yet; beta players can contact the developer through TestFlight. Optional analytics remains off unless you opt in.

The game works without analytics

Prism Duel is a single-player puzzle duel. No account, advertising, purchases with real money, cross-app tracking, camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access is required. Progress, earned coins, cosmetic equipment, settings, local daily-run dates and best scores are stored on your device. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your device backups. No cloud save or online duels are included in this beta.

Optional analytics and crash reports

“Share anonymous analytics” is off by default. First launch offers equally prominent Yes and No thanks choices; either leads to the same game. You can change your choice in Settings. If you turn it on and the developer has configured the service, Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help us understand game balance and diagnose failures. Without that configuration nothing is sent by this feature. Turning it off stops future collection, resets the local analytics identifier/data and discards unsent crash reports; it does not promise to erase reports already received by the service.

Events describe tutorial progress, match starts/results, rival and stage, move counts, remaining HP, stars, active match duration, combos, specials, Prism Slash uses, Rush score/tier, unlocked stages, setting changes and earned-coin cosmetic purchases. A feedback event contains only a rating and whether a written note exists. We never include the note, an email address, a player name or a custom user ID in analytics.

Firebase may process app-instance/installation identifiers, app and operating-system versions, device characteristics, crash stacks and approximate geographic information derived from network connections. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not a guarantee of irreversible anonymity. We use the Analytics Core product without advertising-identifier support, disable IDFV collection and advertising consent/personalization, and do not use analytics for advertising or cross-company tracking. IP addresses are needed to deliver network requests; the game never requests your GPS location.

Google operates the processing service. See Firebase privacy and security and Google’s privacy policy. The developer must configure a two-month Analytics event-data retention period before the beta. Crash/report retention follows the configured Firebase service and its documented policy; do not publish an unverified shorter retention promise. Access to dashboards is restricted to the developer. Data may be processed outside your country.

Feedback

The in-game feedback card stores at most 20 notes of up to 1,000 characters locally. You can delete them in Settings. An automatic invitation appears after three completed duels, then no more than once per seven days; it is optional. Settings lets you open feedback whenever you choose.

The current beta has no configured support mailbox. Saved notes are not automatically delivered to the developer. TestFlight’s own feedback function is a separate Apple service. Once a support address is configured, an Email feedback button opens a draft in your mail app; you decide whether to send it. Emailing voluntarily shares your message and sender address, and this policy/privacy label must be reviewed when that route is enabled. Avoid personal or sensitive information in notes.

Your choices and contact

You can play with analytics off, switch it off later, delete saved feedback, and use TestFlight to contact the developer during the beta. There is no advertising-tracking permission prompt. This game is not submitted in Apple’s Made for Kids category.

Before the public App Store release, this page will be updated with the developer’s legal identity, support contact and verified service-retention details. Requests concerning data already received should go to that contact; because the game does not collect your name or account, locating an individual anonymous/pseudonymous report may not be possible without additional information you choose to provide.
